## About

I’m **Vaibhav Garg**, a software-focused builder working at the intersection of **software engineering, artificial intelligence, cloud infrastructure, automation, and product development**.

My journey started in operations and technology-driven business environments, where I developed a strong understanding of real-world workflows, data, customers, and operational systems. That experience gradually evolved into a deeper focus on **software engineering and AI**, with an emphasis on building practical systems rather than learning technology in isolation.

Today, I focus on building **full-stack applications, AI-powered products, intelligent automation workflows, cloud-based systems, and modern digital experiences**.

I enjoy working across the entire product lifecycle — from understanding the problem and designing the architecture to writing code, integrating AI, deploying infrastructure, optimizing performance, and improving the final product.

### Engineering Philosophy

> **Understand the problem. Design the system. Build the product. Automate the repetitive. Improve continuously.**
